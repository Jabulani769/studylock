# 00 — Product Brief

## Vision
StudyLock helps a student consistently study and prepare for examinations by combining an automatically managed academic plan with a protected, ADHD-friendly focus session.

## Target platform
- Android mobile application.
- Local-first operation; essential planning and session records must work offline.
- Initial target is the owner's own Android phone. Supported Android versions and device/OEM compatibility remain open decisions.

## Core promise
On each eligible weekday, StudyLock prepares and guides one continuous two-hour study session. It automatically selects the module, topic, learning activity and due revision based on the syllabus, academic phase, assessment history and exam information currently available.

## Product pillars
1. **Academic intelligence:** balanced syllabus coverage, revision, assessment and exam preparation.
2. **Progressive data readiness:** start with partial data; incorporate outlines and official dates later.
3. **Focus enforcement:** reduce access to distractions during a session within Android's real technical and policy limits.
4. **ADHD-friendly experience:** reduce decisions, keep the next action visible, support attention resets and avoid shame or punishment.
5. **Trust and explainability:** show why a task was selected, what is known versus estimated, and what the app can and cannot enforce.

## Confirmed owner decisions
- Lockdown ambition: extreme.
- Normal session: one continuous 120-minute session.
- Study days: Monday–Friday, subject to availability and calendar exceptions.
- Saturday and Sunday: complete rest days.
- Topic and module selection: automatic.
- Midsem target: half of each module's topics, rounded up for odd topic counts.
- The syllabus sequence is the default ordering for the first-half target.
- Midsem preparation must not wait for the official midsem timetable.
- Academic data is received progressively, often after semester start.
- No invented topics, official dates or exam facts.
- Endsem examination structure confirmed: Section A = 2 compulsory ×20; Section B = 5 offered, choose 3 ×20; total answered marks 100.
- The app is designed primarily around the user's ADHD-related attention needs.

## Non-goals for initial release
Unless separately approved, do not add:
- social feeds, leaderboards or public profiles;
- cloud accounts or multi-device sync;
- AI-generated syllabus content presented as official lecturer content;
- automatic lecture recording or transcription;
- invasive monitoring such as keystroke capture or continuous screen recording;
- monetization or advertising;
- iOS or desktop applications.

## Success measures
- The student can start a useful plan before all outlines or exam dates arrive.
- The app maintains accurate topic coverage and learning history after new data arrives.
- Daily assignments are understandable and realistically completable in 120 minutes.
- Weak topics receive appropriate additional practice and revision.
- The student can recover from missed sessions without punitive overload.
- Enforcement behavior is measured and described accurately on supported devices.
- The experience minimizes unnecessary decisions and supports returning to study after attention lapses.
